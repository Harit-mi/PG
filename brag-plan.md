# Brag Plan: OUR-PG SaaS

1. **What is the app?**
   A complete SaaS platform for PG and Hostel owners to digitally manage tenants, rooms, rent collection, and publishing weekly food menus to a mobile PWA.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "Complete hostel management from one dashboard. You built it. Now collect the rent."

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   The visual Room Board with interactive beds turning from green (Vacant) to red (Occupied).

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   The Room Board visualization, the Dues/Rent register, and the Tenant Portal (mobile view) showing the weekly food menu and DPDP erasure button.

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   20 seconds. (Hook -> Room Board -> Tenant Portal -> Share CTA).

6. **What tone fits best?**
   `app-store`: Smooth, feature-card clean, professional SaaS energy.

7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Warm corporate SaaS launch track with clean, satisfying UI notification SFX.

8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "Manage your PG hostel completely digitally—from visual room tracking to automated rent collection and daily food menus. Try the OUR-PG SaaS today."

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Owner assigns a room on the visual board -> Rent is automatically generated -> Tenant views their food menu and rent due on the mobile web app.

---

## Storyboard (20 seconds)

- **0:00 - 0:03 | Hook:** 
  Fast zoom into the main dashboard. Text overlay: "Running a PG just got easy."
- **0:03 - 0:08 | The Room Board:** 
  Pan across the visual Room Board. A vacant bed is clicked and assigned to a tenant. It turns red. Text: "Visual Room Management"
- **0:08 - 0:13 | Automated Billing:** 
  Cut to the Dues register. The rent cron job flashes "Generated". Text: "Automated Rent Invoices"
- **0:13 - 0:17 | Tenant PWA:** 
  Slide in a mobile phone frame showing the Tenant Portal. Scroll through the Weekly Food Menu and DPDP compliance. Text: "Mobile Tenant Portal"
- **0:17 - 0:20 | Outro/CTA:** 
  Logo resolves in center. Text: "OUR-PG SaaS. Launching Today."
