# Taste Skill: Anti-Slop Frontend & UI/UX Design System

> High-taste, anti-slop frontend rule for landing pages, web apps, portals, and UI redesigns. Shipped interfaces must look distinct, intentional, and non-generic.

---

## 0. Brief Inference (Read the Room First)
Before writing frontend code or adjusting styling, state a one-line **Design Read**:
> *"Reading this as: <page kind> for <audience>, with a <vibe> language, leaning toward <design system/aesthetic family>."*

---

## 1. The Three Dials
Every layout, motion, and visual decision is driven by three dials:
- **`DESIGN_VARIANCE`**: 1 (Strict Symmetry) to 10 (Artsy Chaos) — *Default: 7*
- **`MOTION_INTENSITY`**: 1 (Static) to 10 (Physics/Cinematic) — *Default: 6*
- **`VISUAL_DENSITY`**: 1 (Airy/Art Gallery) to 10 (Packed Cockpit) — *Default: 4*

---

## 2. Anti-Default Discipline
Avoid generic LLM UI tropes:
- ❌ NO AI-purple gradients or purple-on-black neon glow defaults.
- ❌ NO centered hero section over dark mesh background with 3 identical cards below it.
- ❌ NO unstyled `Inter + slate-900` fallback combo.
- ❌ NO generic blurred glassmorphism smeared everywhere without clear contrast boundaries.

---

## 3. High-Taste Design Requirements
- **Typography & Scale**: Pair distinct display fonts with readable body type. Use strict modular font scales and tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`) for currency, numbers, and stats.
- **Color Systems**: Define clear semantic surface, border, brand, and muted tokens. Maintain WCAG AAA/AA color contrast standards for readability.
- **Layout & Rhythm**: Asymmetric grids, distinct visual anchors, and dynamic card layouts over cookie-cutter equal boxes.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Smooth state transitions, subtle hover elevations, active press scales, and accessible focus outlines.
- **Data Display & Affordances**: Clear empty states with contextual icons and actionable primary triggers.
